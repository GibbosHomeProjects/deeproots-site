Privacy Policy

Deep Roots (the “Game”) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Adam Gibson (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us at privacy@deeproots.example.

2. What the Game collects

InformationWhy
Game progress (upgrades, currencies, settings). Stored on your device. To let you continue playing.
Gameplay and usage data: sessions, levels reached, purchases, and ad events, linked to a random identifier rather than your name. To understand how the Game is used, fix problems and balance difficulty.
Device information: device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address. To make the Game work on your device and to show relevant ads.
Advertising identifier (Apple’s IDFA). Only if you allow tracking when asked. To show more relevant ads and measure whether they worked.
Purchase information: which products were bought. We never see your card or payment details; Apple handles payment. To unlock what you bought and restore it on request.

We do not ask you for your name, email address or phone number to play, and we do not collect your precise location or contacts.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and is supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases. Ads are served through an advertising mediation service, which runs an auction between several ad networks. To do this, these partners receive technical data from your device such as your IP address, device type, ad interactions and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier.

The services we plan to use are listed below. We will keep this list up to date whenever we change them.

  • AppLovin MAX (mediation) and the ad networks it connects us to — see AppLovin’s privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob — see Google’s privacy policy.
  • Apple (App Store, in-app purchases) — see Apple’s privacy policy.

These companies act as independent controllers of the data they receive for advertising. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers and we do not knowingly work with partners whose business is reselling it.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS). When asked, you can decline tracking. The Game works the same, but ads may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (UK and EU). Where the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent before using personal data for advertising. You can change your choice from the Game’s settings screen.
  • Personalised ads. You can limit ad personalisation in your device’s privacy settings.
  • Remove ads. An optional purchase removes forced ads.

5. Legal basis (UK and EU users)

We rely on consent for advertising and any use of your advertising identifier, and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the Game for basic analytics. We rely on contract to deliver purchases you make.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age for consent, that age applies.

7. How long we keep data

We keep analytics data for as long as it is useful to run and improve the Game, and no longer than needed. Data held by our advertising partners is subject to their own policies.

8. Where data goes

Some of our partners are based outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those partners put in place, such as approved standard contractual clauses.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how it is used, to receive a copy, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email privacy@deeproots.example. Because we do not know who you are by name, we may ask you for the Game’s random player identifier so we can find your data.

If you are in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). If you are in the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the Game.
